26:Size each subagent to its work - reasoning effort and how much of the ticket it is handed - and, where your harness lets you choose a subagent's model, name the model explicitly on every call instead of letting it inherit yours: the cheapest tier (haiku) for search, grep, listing, and file-reading sweeps; the middle tier (sonnet) for per-item inventories, classification, and routine implementation; the strongest model only for synthesis, design, or genuinely ambiguous judgment, which you usually keep for yourself rather than hand out.
